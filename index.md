### 2. Google Play Games Level Up & Cloud Saves
Rex & Rebels: Beat 'Em Up participates in the Google Play Games Level Up program. To provide a seamless, cross-device gaming experience, our app integrates the **Play Games Services v2 SDK**. 

This allows for:
*   **Platform Authentication:** Linking your gameplay to your Google Play profile.
*   **Cloud Saves:** Storing your game progress, unlocked characters, and in-game currency on remote servers so you never lose your progress across devices.
*   **Game Stats and Achievements:** Tracking your milestones and interactions to power leaderboards, quests, and rewards.

**What we collect:** Gameplay progress, high scores, device identifiers, and account identifiers linked via Google Play.
**Why we collect it:** To fulfill the functional requirements of cloud saving, cross-device compatibility, and the social features of the Play Games ecosystem.

In compliance with Google Play’s Data Safety and Account Deletion requirements, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and any associated cloud save data. 

If you wish to delete your account, your cloud saves, and associated gameplay data, you can do so by:
1.  **In-App Deletion:** Navigating to the Settings menu within the game and selecting "Delete Account/Data."

Upon receiving a request, all associated gameplay data, cloud saves, and personal identifiers linked to your account will be permanently deleted from our active servers within 30 days, unless retention is strictly required for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes.
